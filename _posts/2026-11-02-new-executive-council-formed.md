The Dhaka University Alumni Association of Michigan has formed a new five-member executive council, unanimously electing economist and educator **Professor Dr. Ahsan Habib**, a former administrator at Adrian College, as president.

| Position | Name |
| --- | --- |
| President | Professor Dr. Ahsan Habib |
| Vice President | Iqbal Faiz Sapon |
| General Secretary | Saria Sadiq |
| Joint General Secretary | Mohammad Matin |
| Treasurer | Faisal Ahmed |

Members thanked the previous term's chief convener, Aziz Chowdhury, and conveners Syed Reza and Iqbal Faiz for their contributions to the association.

A total of 17 members were present at the meeting: Dr. Hafizuddin, Dr. Marufa Rahman, Professor Dr. Ahsan Habib, Professor Nazmul Islam, Aziz Chowdhury, Syed Reza, MD. Zubairul Chowdhury Khokon, Professor Mohammad Tamizur Rahman, Dr. Syed Ashraf, Dr. MD Musharraf Hossain, Dr. Shah Alam Bhuiyan, Professor Dr. Messer Ali, Iqbal Faiz Sapon (Histologist), Mohammad Matin (Pharmacist), Dr. Mohammad Muktadir Talukder, Saria Sadiq (Biochemist) and Faisal Ahmed.
